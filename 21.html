01 / Discover

History meets endless energy.

Osaka is famous for its lively atmosphere, outgoing locals, and rich culinary history. From the historic majesty of Osaka Castle to the bustling neon-lit canals of Dotonbori, every corner is full of character.

Known as "Kuidaore" (eat until you drop), the city offers an unmatched street food culture that keeps visitors coming back for more.

02 / Neighborhoods

Neon lights & Retro charms.

Explore Shinsekai for a taste of retro Osaka, dominated by the Tsutenkaku Tower, or dive into Shinsaibashi for world-class shopping and modern fashion.

Take time to stroll through Kuromon Market in the morning, savoring freshly grilled seafood, takoyaki, and local delicacies.
